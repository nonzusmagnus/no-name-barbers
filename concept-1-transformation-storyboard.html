No Name Barbers — Concept 1

The Transformation

FULL SCRIPT STORYBOARD • SINGLE-CLIENT ARC → SHOP MONTAGE → LINE-UP FINALE
♫ Jazz — moody, confident, daytime lounge. Not upbeat.
ATMOSPHERE: BRIGHT DAYTIME — natural daylight through storefront, sunlit shop, clean sharp shadows
Opening

Arrival & First Cut

~0:00 – 0:25
Voiceover"There's people out there that go to just any barber. Me? I need the best of the best. Not the ones where the haircut fades quickly or is gone after a week. The one that actually lasts, the one that doesn't need all the enhancers. When I get in, I like to have something that turns heads."
[ Door DAY — sunlight in, client walks in ]

Shot 1 — The Entrance

Daytime. Camera picks client up at the sunlit door, tracks him toward the chair. Daylight streams through storefront. Shop ambience, low jazz.

[ Settle in — greet + prep ]

Shot 2 — Settle In

He settles in, barber greets him, starts prepping tools. Cape on, tools laid out.

[ Clippers — first pass ]

Shot 3 — Cut Begins

First pass with clippers. Close detail shots, hair falling, hands steady.

[ Mirror — barber focused ]

Shot 4 — Reflection

Mirror reflection, barber focused in. Eye contact, precision.

Midpoint

Multi-Chair Montage

~0:25 – 0:50
DIRECTION: Shift into the wider shop. Cut across the room to the rhythm of the jazz, not fast-cut. Let each barber hold screen. Hands, tools, focus, technique.
Voiceover"It's not just about looking good for the day. I need something that holds up, whether I'm out at night or going through my week. That's the difference between a barber and a real one."
[ Full shop DAY — sunlit, all chairs working ]

Shot 5 — The Shop Alive

Bright daytime. Multiple barbers, multiple chairs, multiple clients, all working at once. Natural daylight, clean sharp look.

[ Chair 1 — clippers ]

Shot 6 — Clippers

Clippers at one chair. Fade work, detail.

[ Chair 2 — straight razor ]

Shot 7 — Straight Razor

Straight razor at another. Slow, deliberate strokes.

[ Chair 3 — comb-out ]

Shot 8 — Comb-Out

A comb-out at a third. Finish and finesse. Give each barber a moment.

Resolution

The Reveal

~0:50 – 1:10
Voiceover"When I walk out of here, I don't just feel fresh. I feel sharp. That's the only kind of barber worth going to."
[ Line-up — final details ]

Shot 9 — Final Details

Back to original client. Cut finishing, final details, line-up, brush-off.

[ Mirror check — nod ]

Shot 10 — Approval

He checks himself in the mirror. Slight nod or smirk. Satisfied.

[ Walk out DAY — into daylight ]

Shot 11 — Walk Out

He gets up, walks out into bright daylight. Confidence. Hold on sunlit door.

[ Barbers line up — camera ]

Shot 12 — The Line-Up

All barbers line up together inside the shop, looking straight at camera. Jazz resolves.
